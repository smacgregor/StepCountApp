# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 2, 2026  
**App:** StepCount

## Overview

StepCount is an Apple Watch app that displays your daily step count using data from Apple Health (HealthKit).

Your privacy is important. StepCount is designed to work with your health data locally on your Apple devices. We do not operate a server that collects or stores your personal health information.

## Information We Access

With your permission, StepCount accesses the following information through Apple's HealthKit framework:

- **Step Count:** Used to calculate and display your daily step total and progress toward your step goal.

StepCount only requests access to the health information necessary to provide its step-counting functionality.

StepCount does not write health information to Apple Health.

## How Your Information Is Used

Your step-count information is used exclusively to:

- Display your daily step count in the StepCount Apple Watch app.
- Display your step count in supported watch complications and widgets.
- Help you monitor your daily activity and progress toward your step goal.

Your HealthKit information is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the app's functionality.

## Data Storage and Sharing

StepCount processes HealthKit information locally on your Apple device.

- We do not transmit your HealthKit information to external servers.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your health information with third parties.
- We do not use health information for advertising or marketing.
- We do not maintain user accounts or require you to register to use StepCount.

Apple manages the storage and security of information in Apple Health according to Apple's own privacy policies and security practices.

## Third-Party Services

StepCount does not integrate third-party analytics or advertising services that collect your health information.

Apple's operating systems and HealthKit framework are used to provide the app's functionality. Your use of Apple Health and related Apple services is also subject to Apple's privacy policies.

## Data Retention and Deletion

StepCount does not maintain a separate server-side copy of your health information.

Your step-count records remain in Apple Health and are managed through Apple's Health app. You can revoke StepCount's access at any time:

1. Open the Health app on your iPhone.
2. Tap your profile picture.
3. Select **Apps and Services** (or **Apps**, depending on your iOS version).
4. Select **StepCount**.
5. Change or revoke its access to your health data.

You can also remove StepCount from your Apple Watch. Removing the app does not delete your underlying step-count records from Apple Health.

## Children's Privacy

StepCount does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. The app does not require an account or request personally identifying information.
