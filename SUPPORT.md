# StepCount Support

Thanks for using StepCount!

StepCount is a simple Apple Watch app that displays your daily step count using data from Apple Health.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why isn't my step count appearing?

Make sure StepCount has permission to read your step count from Apple Health.

On your iPhone:

1. Open the Health app.
2. Tap your profile picture.
3. Select Apps and Services.
4. Select StepCount.
5. Make sure access to Step Count is enabled.

### Why isn't my complication updating?

Apple Watch complications and widgets refresh according to watchOS scheduling. Updates may not appear immediately. Opening StepCount can help trigger a refresh.

### Does StepCount collect or share my health information?

No. StepCount reads your step-count information from Apple Health to display your daily activity. Your health information is processed locally and is not transmitted to our servers.

For more information, see our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).
